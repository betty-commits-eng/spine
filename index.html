How IndieShelf Works
Three steps from "what are we reading?" to a shelf everyone can browse.
1

Sign in with Google

No new password to remember — your club's organizer adds your email, and you're in.

2

Add a book in seconds

Start typing a title and IndieShelf finds the author, year and cover for you automatically.

3

Browse, rate & discuss

See the whole club's shelves, rate what you've read, and leave notes for each other.
